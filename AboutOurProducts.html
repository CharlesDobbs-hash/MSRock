Our products start out as unremarkable rocks. Our practiced eyes can see the beauty they will become. It does take a lot of work to get them to resemble our vision. They are cut, polished, burnished, drilled, and mounted. The process can take from four to eight weeks. It is a lot of work, but it is done with the love of creating unique pieces.

We started out as rock hounds, traveling all over the West looking for unusual stones for our garden. Once our garden was full, we started looking for other ways to use our collection. What started as a small rock shop has grown into a large shed with a multitude of tools to bring out the hidden beauty of nature's bounty.
