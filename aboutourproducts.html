Our Story

Video coming soon

Our products can start out as unremarkable. Thankfully, many times, we can see the beauty they will become. Although it does take a lot of thought and work to get them to resemble our vision, they are cut, polished, burnished, drilled and created. This process can take anywhere from four to eight weeks. It is a lot of work but is done with the love of creating unique pieces.

We started out as rockhounds, traveling all over, looking for unusual stones for our rock garden. Once our garden was full, we started thinking of other ways to use our collection. What started out as a small garden with cool rocks turned into a closet with a couple of lapidary machines. Today, we have grown to producing approximately 11 different products and progressed into an actual lapidary shop!

God’s hand is in all that is created and life is good!
